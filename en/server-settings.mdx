---
title: "Server settings"
description: "What you can change on a Cevexa Server or Department: general info, parent, type conversion and deletion."
---

The **Settings** page of a Server or Department holds everything you can
change about it. What you see depends on your role — most actions need
**administrator** or **owner**.

## General

Administrators and owners can edit:

- **Name** — 1 to 100 characters, required.
- **Description** — optional, up to 1000 characters.
- **Icon URL** — optional, an `https` link to a square image shown across
  Cevexa.

Press **Save** to apply or **Discard** to undo your edits.

## Parent Server (Departments only)

A Department's settings show its **parent**:

- **Change parent** / **Attach to a Server** — pick a Server you administer
  to link the Department under it.
- **Detach** — remove the parent link; the Department becomes independent
  and keeps its members and integrations.

Regular Servers don't have this section — only Departments can have a
parent.

## Type conversion

Owners can convert the object between **Server** and **Department**:

- **Convert to Department** — the Server becomes a Department; you can link
  it to a parent now or later. If the Server still has Departments, the
  button is blocked until they are detached or moved.
- **Convert to Server** — the Department becomes a top-level Server and
  loses its parent link.

Conversion keeps everything: the object's identity, members and
integrations stay exactly as they were.

## Danger zone

**Delete Server / Delete Department** permanently removes the object, its
members and its integrations. To confirm you must type the object's name.

Deleting a **Server that has Departments** does not delete them — they are
detached first and become independent Departments.

Only the **owner** can delete an object.

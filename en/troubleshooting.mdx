description: "Fix common problems: missing guilds, stuck connections, sign-in loops and error messages."

## "Log in with Discord again" keeps appearing

Your guild list is refreshed each time you sign in and stays valid for 24
hours. When it expires — or right before connecting a guild — Cevexa asks
you to sign in again. This is normal: press the button, re-authorize with
Discord, and continue where you left off.

## My guild doesn't appear in the picker

Check, in order:

1. You can manage the guild in Discord — owner, Administrator or Manage
   Server. Guilds you cannot manage are hidden (the picker tells you how
   many were hidden).
2. You signed in **after** joining the guild or getting the permission —
   sign in again to refresh the list.
3. The guild isn't already connected to a Cevexa object — connected guilds
   are marked and can't be picked. Disconnect it there first.

## "This guild is already connected"

A Discord guild can only be linked to one Cevexa Server or Department at a
time. Objects with Discord connected are marked in your Servers list —
open the object, choose **Discord** under **Platform**, then
**Integrations**, disconnect it there, and connect it where you want it.

## The bot install step is stuck on "Waiting for the bot to join…"

- Make sure you completed the Discord authorization in the popup.
- Check the bot actually landed in the guild (it should appear in the
  member list).
- Wait a few seconds — detection can take a moment.
- If it still doesn't move, cancel and start again; or resume it from
  **Pending connections** on the main dashboard page.

## "The connection expired"

An unfinished connection lasts 15 minutes. Start it again — or, if the bot
is now installed, resume it from **Pending connections** on the main
dashboard page and it will complete immediately.

## "You can't manage this Discord guild"

Cevexa only lets you connect guilds where you are the owner or have
Administrator / Manage Server. If you believe you do, sign in again — your
permissions may be newer than your last sign-in.

## "This Server still has Departments"

You can't convert or delete a Server while it has Departments attached.
Open **Departments**, detach or move them first, then retry. (For deletes,
you can also choose the option that detaches them automatically.)

## "Not found or no access"

You are not a member of that object — objects you can't access don't show
up at all. Ask the owner or an administrator of that Server or Department
for access.

## "This action isn't allowed from here"

The action was started from an unexpected place. Use the dashboard directly
at [cevexa.xyz](https://cevexa.xyz) and try again — don't start actions from
third-party pages or copied links.

## "The service is temporarily unavailable"

Something on Cevexa's side is having a moment. Wait a minute and retry; if
it persists, check [status.cevexa.xyz](https://status.cevexa.xyz) or ask in
the [support Discord](https://cevexa.xyz/discord).

## "Couldn't load this page" / network errors

Check your connection and retry. If only one page fails while others work,
note what you clicked and report it in the support Discord.

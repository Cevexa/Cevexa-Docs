---
title: "Servers and Departments"
description: "How Cevexa organizes communities: Servers, Departments, parents, ownership and roles."
---

Cevexa organizes everything into **Servers** and **Departments**. Both are
full, independent Cevexa objects — the difference is only how they relate to
each other.

## Server

A **Server** is the main Cevexa object — think of it as your community. It
has its own name, description, icon, owner, members, settings and connected
platforms (like a Discord guild).

A Discord guild is **not** automatically a Cevexa Server. A guild is an
external thing you *connect* to a Server — the same way you might connect
other platforms later.

## Department

A **Department** is the same kind of object as a Server — it has everything a
Server has: its own name, icon, owner, members, settings and its own Discord
connection. Departments are for parts of your community that should be
managed separately (a sub-team, a division, a district).

A Department:

- works fine on its own, with no parent;
- can be linked under **one** Server (its *parent*);
- cannot have Departments of its own — Departments don't nest;
- can be moved to a different parent or detached at any time — it keeps all
  its members and connections.

When you detach a Department it simply becomes independent — nothing is
deleted.

## What parents do (and don't do)

Linking a Department to a parent Server is only an organizational link:

- Members of the parent can *see* the Department in the parent's Departments
  list.
- Members of the parent do **not** get any access inside the Department —
  Department membership is still managed separately.
- The parent's owner does **not** own the Department.
- Moving a Department to a new parent never moves its members or owner.
- Deleting a Server never deletes its Departments — they are detached first
  and stay usable.

## Ownership and roles

Every Server and Department has exactly one **owner** — the person who
created it. Everyone else is a member with a role:

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | Everything: edit settings, manage integrations and Departments, convert the type, delete the object |
| **Administrator** | Edit settings, manage integrations, create/attach/detach Departments |
| **Member** | View the object, its members and its integrations |

Roles never carry over: being the owner of a parent Server gives you no
extra rights in its Departments, and installing the Cevexa bot in your guild
never makes you the owner of anything. See
[Members and roles](/en/members-and-roles).

## Server or Department?

- **Server → Department:** the owner can convert a Server into a Department.
  If it still has Departments of its own, detach or move them first — the
  conversion is blocked until then.
- **Department → Server:** the owner can convert a Department into a
  top-level Server; its parent link is removed.

Conversion keeps everything — the object's identity, members and connected
platforms stay the same. You'll find it in the object's settings.

## Connected platforms

A Server or Department can be connected to platform resources. Today Cevexa
supports **Discord** — one Discord guild per Server or Department, and each
guild can only be connected to one Cevexa object at a time. ER:LC support is
planned; the dashboard already shows it as "not available yet".

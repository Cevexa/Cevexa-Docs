---
title: "Members and roles"
description: "Who can see and change what on a Cevexa Server or Department."
---

Every Cevexa Server and Department has its **members** — the people who can
open it in the dashboard. Each member has one role. You can see the member
list on the object's **Members** page.

## Roles

| Role | Can do |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | Everything. Manage settings, integrations and Departments, convert the object's type, delete it. Every object has exactly one owner — the person who created it. |
| **Administrator** | Edit the object's settings (name, description, icon), connect and disconnect Discord, create Departments, attach and detach them. |
| **Member** | View the object: its overview, members list, Departments and integrations. |

## What membership means

- Membership belongs to **one** Server or Department. Being a member of a
  Server does not make you a member of its Departments, and being an owner
  or administrator of a parent gives no rights inside a Department.
- If you are not a member of an object, it does not appear in your
  dashboard at all — you will not see its name or that it exists.
- Members of a parent Server can open its Departments list and see each
  Department's card (name, icon, whether Discord is connected) plus whether
  they have a role in it — but nothing inside the Department itself.

## Changing roles

Role management in the dashboard is not available yet — the Members page
today shows the list of members and their roles. **Not implemented:** adding
members or changing roles from the UI. The person who creates a Server or
Department is its owner automatically.

## What is the difference between a Discord guild and a Cevexa Server?

A Discord guild lives in Discord. A Cevexa Server is your community's object
in Cevexa — it can be *connected* to one Discord guild, but it also has its
own members, roles and settings. Deleting one never deletes the other.

## How many Discord guilds can I connect?

One guild per Server or Department — and each guild can only be connected to
one Cevexa object at a time. If your guild is already linked somewhere else,
disconnect it there first: objects with Discord connected are marked in the
Servers list — open the object, choose **Discord** under **Platform**, then
**Integrations** → **Disconnect**.

## I was invited to a guild but can't see it in the picker. Why?

The guild picker only shows guilds you can *manage* — where you are the
owner, or have Administrator or Manage Server permission. Also make sure
you've signed in after joining the guild: your guild list updates on every
sign-in.

## Does the Cevexa bot need permissions in my guild?

No. The invite asks for no special permissions — the bot only needs to be in
the guild for the connection to work and to answer its commands.

## What happens if the bot is kicked from my guild?

Your connection is kept and shows as **Bot removed**. Re-add the bot with
**Add Cevexa again** on the Integrations page and it becomes active again —
your members and settings are untouched.

## What's the difference between Standard and Plus?

Cevexa has two bot variants — Standard and Plus. Plus adds extra features on
top of the standard set. If both bots are in your guild, removing one does
not break the connection as long as the other is still installed.

## Can a Department have its own Departments?

No — Departments can't nest. A Department can only be linked under a regular
Server (or exist independently).

## If I delete a Server, do its Departments get deleted too?

No — they are detached and become independent. Deleting a Server only
removes the Server itself, its members and its integrations.

## Is ER:LC supported?

Not yet. The dashboard shows the ER:LC integration as "not available yet" —
it is planned, and your ER:LC connections will appear there when it ships.

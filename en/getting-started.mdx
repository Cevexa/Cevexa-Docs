Cevexa connects your Discord guilds to Servers you manage from one dashboard.
You sign in with your Discord account — there is no separate Cevexa password.

## Sign in with Discord

1. Open [cevexa.xyz/login](https://cevexa.xyz/login) and choose **Log in with Discord**.
2. Authorize Cevexa on the Discord page. Cevexa reads your profile and the
   list of guilds you are in — this is how the dashboard knows which guilds
   you can connect.
3. You land on your **dashboard**.

Your guild list is refreshed every time you sign in. If you joined a new
guild or got new permissions, sign in again so the dashboard sees it. When
you start connecting a guild, Cevexa may also ask you to sign in again if
your last sign-in is older than 24 hours — this is normal and only takes a
moment.

## Create your first Server

On the dashboard, press **Create**. You have three options:

| Option | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Start with Discord** | Pick one of your Discord guilds. A new Server is created and linked to that guild in one step. |
| **Create Server** | A top-level Cevexa community, not linked to Discord. You can connect Discord later. |
| **Create Department** | An independent Cevexa object, optionally linked under a Server you administer. See [Servers and Departments](/en/platform/domain-model). |

**Start with Discord** is the fastest path: choose a guild you manage, add
the Cevexa bot when prompted, and your Server is ready. Details on the
connection step are in [Connect Discord](/en/platform/discord-integration).

## What you can do next

- Manage members and their roles — [Members and roles](/en/members-and-roles)
- Rename your Server, set an icon, attach Departments — [Server settings](/en/server-settings)
- See which of your objects have Discord connected — the **Servers** list on
  the dashboard marks them; the connection itself lives in the object's
  Discord workspace (open it, choose **Discord** under **Platform**, then
  **Integrations**)
- Manage your account and the devices you are signed in on — open your
  profile menu → **Profile**, then **Sessions**
